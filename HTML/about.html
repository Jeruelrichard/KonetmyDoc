Mission Statement

To simplify and personalize the healthcare booking experience for every user by leveraging smart reminders, seamless rescheduling, and inclusive communication—making healthcare more accessible, inclusive, and patient-first.

Vision Statement

To become the leading digital health platform that redefines how people connect with care—by blending innovation, empathy, and efficiency to improve outcomes and patient satisfaction across all age groups and backgrounds.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect:

  • Personal details (name, contact info, gender, birthday)
  • Health-related preferences and appointment history
  • Device data and usage activity (for analytics & optimization)

How we use Your Information:

  • To send reminders via SMS, email, or in-app
  • To personalize appointment suggestions and autofill options
  • For communication, service improvement, and rescheduling

Data Security:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Role-based access for healthcare providers
  • Regular audits to maintain privacy standards

Your Rights:

  • Access or modify your data
  • Opt-out of communication
  • Delete your account and associated data

Terms of Service

User Responsibilities:

  • Ensure accuracy of personal data
  • Show up or cancel/reschedule in advance
  • Use platform features responsibly

Service Commitments:

  • Timely and relevant health-related reminders
  • Transparent doctor availability, especially weekends
  • Easy-to-use interface for all age groups using inclusive language

Booking Terms:

  • Rescheduling available up to [X hours/days] before appointment
  • One-tap confirmation and cancellation options provided
  • Doctors are assigned considering availability and demand patterns
